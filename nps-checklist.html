Closing spiels

Spiel Recommendation

Use these before ending the call, matched to what you noticed during the modification.

Before closing spiel (contact forms)

"Would there be any other changes you'd like to make on the site? (if none) that's great! Before we end this call, I've noticed that you have contact forms on your site, I hope you are receiving the form responses. You can actually receive those on your Business Center/Marketing Center/My Account (formerly Command Center). The good thing about that is the contact information of those who filled out the forms automatically gets saved there. That way after the service you've provided them, you can request for reviews, which we can display on your site."

Before closing spiel (website editor access)

"Would there be any other changes you'd like to make on the site? (if none) Great! Before we end this call, we appreciate your interest in learning to make changes on the site yourself. If you have the time, you can also access this website editor on your Business Center/Marketing Center/My Account (formerly Command Center). If you want to make some edits and you need assistance in doing so, please give us a call and we'll definitely walk you through it."

Before closing spiel (scheduling or booking set up)

"Would there be any other changes you'd like to make on the site? (if none) Great! Before we end this call, while editing your site earlier, I've noticed that you offer multiple services on your site. If you don't have a booking or scheduling system set up yet, we have that option available for you on your Business Center. I can connect you over to our Thryv Support team to have that set up for you and they can also talk to you about the other features that your business can benefit from. If you have the time, I can connect you over to them or I can give you their phone number, so you can reach out to them when you're available. Which would you prefer?"

Before closing spiel (scheduling or booking set up — alternate)

"Would there be any other changes you'd like to make on the site? (if none) Great! Before we end this call, while editing your site earlier, I've noticed that you offer multiple services on your site. I'm sure sometimes scheduling those appointments gets hectic. You actually have a scheduling/booking feature available on your Business Center. Our Thryv Support team can help you set this up. If you have the time, I can connect you over to them or I can give you their phone number, so you can reach out to them when you're available. Which would you prefer?"

Before closing spiel (offering redesign)

"Would there be any other changes you'd like to make on the site? (if none) Great! Before we end this call, we've done multiple changes on your site today, if you are interested, we'd like to offer a redesign of your site, where a dedicated consultant will speak with you on everything you want to refresh on your site. Not to worry, this is available for you with no additional cost. (if yes) Perfect! I can connect you with our Thryv Support to schedule that consultation, would that be okay with you?"

Before closing spiel (offering redesign — client will think about it)

"Would there be any other changes you'd like to make on the site? (if none) Great! Before we end this call, we've done multiple changes on your site today, if you are interested, we'd like to offer a redesign of your site, where a dedicated consultant will speak with you on everything you want to refresh on your site. Not to worry, this is available for you with no additional cost. (if they say they'll think about it) That's great Mr./Ms. (Client) let me give you this link, where you can check and see some Thryv showcase sites that you might want to consider for that possible refresh — websites.thryv.info."

Before closing spiel (offering redesign — not today)

"Would there be any other changes you'd like to make on the site? (if none) Great! Before we end this call, we've done multiple changes on your site today, if you are interested, we'd like to offer a free redesign of your site, where a dedicated consultant will speak with you everything you want to refresh on your site. Not to worry, this is available for you with no additional cost. (if they say not today) Let me give you this link, where you can check and see some Thryv showcase sites, just in case — websites.thryv.info."
